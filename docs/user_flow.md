# User Flow

## Reader

1. Open the home feed.
2. Swipe through short-form news cards.
3. Inspect the prediction badge and credibility meter.
4. Open the verify page to match the story hash against the ledger.
5. Save or report the story.

## Admin

1. Sign in with an admin account.
2. Upload a story in the dashboard.
3. Review the predicted label and credibility score.
4. Publish the story into the feed.
5. Track community reports and newsroom activity.
